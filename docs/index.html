Common questions

How do people join?
Every group has a six-character invite code, found on the Home tab. Share it, and they enter it after installing the app. Trips have their own separate codes for people outside the group.
Do I need an account or password?
No. Kinvoy creates an anonymous account on your device automatically. You just type the name you want the group to see.
How does location sharing work?
It's always opt-in. Group sharing stays on until you turn it off. Trip sharing only works during that trip's dates and stops automatically when it ends. Turning either off deletes your location from the server.
Can I be in more than one group?
Yes — your family, the in-laws, a friend group. Tap the group name at the top of the screen to switch between them.
How do I report something or block someone?
Press and hold any message, poll, or photo and choose "Report or block." Blocking hides that person's content everywhere in the app.
How do I delete my data?
Home → your group → Delete my account. This permanently removes your account and everything you've posted.
