Kinvoy Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 July 2026

Kinvoy helps families and groups organize trips and daily life together. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and what control you have. Kinvoy is operated by an independent developer, Kendall Sorenson.

What Kinvoy collects

  • The name you type when joining a group or trip. That's the name other members see. It doesn't have to be your legal name.
  • Content you create: messages, calendar events, checklists, expenses, polls, travel details, and photos you upload.
  • Location, only when you turn sharing on. Trip location sharing works only during the trip's dates and stops automatically when the trip ends. Group location sharing stays on until you switch it off. Turning either off deletes your location from our servers — it is not merely hidden.
  • An anonymous account identifier created automatically so the app can remember your groups and trips. Kinvoy does not require an email address, password, or phone number.

What Kinvoy does not collect

  • No advertising identifiers, no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No contacts, no browsing history, no health data.
  • No payment information. Kinvoy is free at this time.

Who can see your information

Only the people in your group or on your trip. Content is protected by database row-level security, so someone who isn't a member of a group cannot read its messages, photos, locations, or plans. Photos are stored in a private bucket and served through short-lived links.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

Service providers

  • Supabase hosts the database, authentication, and photo storage.
  • Anthropic processes activity-suggestion requests. Only the destination, trip dates, and any preferences you type are sent — never your messages, photos, or location.
  • Open-Meteo provides weather forecasts from a destination name.
  • Apple MapKit renders maps and destination search on your device.

Children

Kinvoy is designed for families and may be used by children under a parent's supervision, within a group the parent controls. Kinvoy does not knowingly collect personal information from children outside that context, does not show ads, and does not build advertising profiles.

Your controls

  • Turn location sharing off at any time — your location is deleted immediately.
  • Delete your own messages, photos, plans, and expenses.
  • Leave a group or trip, which removes you and your membership.
  • Delete your account and all of your data from inside the app. This is permanent and cannot be undone.
  • Report content and block people whose content you don't want to see.

Data retention

Group and trip content stays until it is deleted by its author, until the group or trip is deleted, or until you delete your account. Reports of objectionable content are retained so repeat abuse can be acted on.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the updated date above will change and the new version will be posted here.
